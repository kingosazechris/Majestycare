Past Projects

Real field work, documented for credibility.

These past projects show Majesty Cares’ journey from direct grassroots relief to a larger child protection and educational continuity mission.

September 7, 2020

Mercy Orphanage Outreach

Majesty Humanitarian Care & Social Welfare Initiative visited Mercy Orphanage to support vulnerable children with food items, care supplies, and welfare assistance.

June 30, 2022

Jos Educational and Orphanage Outreach

Majesty Team gave out 300 writing materials to primary school pupils and also donated food items to Binta Orphanage Home in Jos.

  • 300 writing materials distributed
  • Primary school pupils supported
  • Food items donated to Binta Orphanage Home
  • Children encouraged to continue learning
October 28, 2023

Widows Smile Outreach

The Widows Smile outreach supported vulnerable widows with care, encouragement, and relief materials, reinforcing Majesty Cares’ commitment to women, families, and community dignity.

Ongoing

Community Support at the Point of Need

Majesty Cares continues to support people through small scale interventions, including school fee support, hospital bill support, welfare assistance, and emergency help for the poor and needy.
