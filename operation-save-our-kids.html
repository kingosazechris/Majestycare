Flagship Campaign

Operation Save Our Kids

Kids Belong in School, Not the Bush

Donate to the campaignContact the team

Nigeria’s insecurity, kidnapping threats, poverty, and school disruption are putting vulnerable children at risk. In Abuja’s slums and peripheral communities, many children are being pushed out of classrooms and into unsafe street environments.

Operation Save Our Kids is a 3 to 6 month emergency intervention to protect, feed, equip, counsel, and retain 3,000 vulnerable children in school.

Target Scale

3,000 children across six FCT Area Councils.

Campaign Pillars

Protection, feeding, learning, healing, and retention.

01

Protect and Prepare

  • Security awareness
  • Emergency drills
  • Self defense and survival awareness
  • School safety sensitization
  • Community based safety coordination
02

Feed and Equip

  • School meals
  • Books and textbooks
  • Notebooks and stationery
  • Hygiene kits
  • Learning packs
03

Heal and Restore

  • Trauma informed counseling
  • Emotional support
  • Child safety education
  • Hope building mentorship
04

Retain and Relocate

  • Scholarships
  • School fee support
  • Emergency relocation support
  • Street to school family stipends

Why This Matters Now

The crisis is bigger than one school or one community.

Across Nigeria, insecurity has disrupted education for many children. Reports of school abductions and attacks on education show why local protection and preparedness work is urgent.

Majesty Cares focuses on ethical response. We do not use identifiable photos of kidnapped children for promotion. We use respectful context, credible sources, and real photos from our own humanitarian work.
